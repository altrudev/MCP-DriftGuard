## Independence boundary

MCP DriftGuard is deliberately standalone. Its core verifier does not require DDC, DSR, DDC Assurance Lab, an LLM, a hosted account, or Altru.dev infrastructure.

DDC Assurance Lab is a related assurance practice founded by Valentyn Rukhaylo. That relationship identifies provenance and organizational context only; it does not imply that a DriftGuard result is a DDCAL certification, accreditation, endorsement by the MCP project, or independent third-party validation.

## Claim discipline

A DriftGuard result concerns the externally visible MCP surface captured by the verifier and the exact baseline supplied. It does not by itself prove that source code, binaries, dependencies, container images, backend behavior, or operational controls are unchanged.

Repository and external claims are version-specific and should be tied to an exact revision or release whenever possible.
